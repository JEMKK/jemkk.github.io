My Routine

I have breakfast at 8:30a.m. while I listen to the radio. I have a dog and two cats. I take my dog for a walk at 9a.m. for 25 minutes. I want to work in a bank because the pay is good, but at the moment I work in a shop. I take the train to work. It takes 30 minutes, so I read the newspaper on the train. When I get to work at 10a.m. I want a coffee, but I the café opens at 11:30 in the morning. I don't eat fast food, so for lunch I eat a sandwich and drink a coffee. At work I have to speak English and Spanish. In the evening I go to Spanish classes. After work, I go back home and watch TV for half an hour. Then I study some Spanish before class. Finally I take the bus to class. I don't have a car. They're too expensive.
