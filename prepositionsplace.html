Prepositions of Place


To talk about a city/country of origin, we use 'from'.
To talk about where we live/work, we use 'in'.
To talk about where we're going, we use 'to'.

Write the correct preposition.

  1. I come Ireland.
  2. I work New York.
  3. Leo Messi comes Argentina
  4. We're going Madrid on holiday.
  5. We live London, but my family comes Manchester.
  6. Rosalía lives Spain.
  7. Tequila comes Mexico.
  8. Hugh Jackman often works the USA.
  9. Superman comes Krypton.
  10. He lives a mansion.
  11. She often goes the cinema at the weekend.
  12. Genghis Khan lived Asia.
  13. We go school from Monday to Friday.
  14. Nelson Mandela was born South Africa.
