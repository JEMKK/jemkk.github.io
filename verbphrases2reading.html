James' Habits

On Wednesdays I do sport with some colleagues from the office. I like tennis, but we usually play football. On Fridays I swim and then I go out with my friends, sometimes we go to a restaurant, others we go to the cinema. At the weekend, I usually stay at home if I'm tired, but this Saturday I'm going to the beach with my parents. I prefer walking in the mountains, but my grandparents have a house on the coast. I'm going to meet some friends there and maybe we'll play computer games to relax. Our friend Peter doesn't like playing video games, but he plays the piano while we play.
