Gazpacho

Ingredients

▢6-7 medium tomatoes
▢1 green Italian pepper
▢1 cucumber
▢½ of a small white onion
▢1 clove garlic
▢¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
▢1 splash sherry vinegar
▢1 pinch salt

Instructions

  • Wash and dry the vegetables.
  • Peel the t, then cut them.
  • Cut the p in half and discard the seeds, then cut it into smaller pieces.
  • Peel the g and cut it in half.
  • Peel and quarter the o.
  • Peel the c and cut it in half. Add to a blender.
  • Add the tomatoes to the blender, then add the pepper, garlic, onion, and cucumber. Blend until it is puréed.
  • Season with s and v. Slowly add the o o.
