Objects Plurals


Most nouns need an 's' for the plural,e.g. a dog=two dogs.
Nouns ending in -x, -ss, -ch, -sh need 'es', e.g. a watch=three watches.
If the noun has two words, the second becomes plural, e.g. a credit card=four credit cards
