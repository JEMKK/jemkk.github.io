Dates


In English, to say the dates we use ordinal numbers.
The first are irregular: one=first; two=second; three=third; four=fourth; five=fifth.

The rest add -th to the end of the number, but there may be some spelling changes *:
six=sixth; seven=seventh; eight=eighth; nine=ninth; ten=tenth.
The tens change the final 'y' and add 'eth', e.g.: twentieth, thirtieth.
