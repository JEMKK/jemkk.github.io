Regular Verbs Write


Regular verbs in the past add '-ed', e.g. walk=walked.If there's already an 'e', don't put another, e.g. love=loved.
Some are semi-regular:
Double the final consonant if:
1.It's a one-syllable word and there's a consonant-vowel-consonant, e.g. stop ('p'=consonant, o=vowel, 't'=consonant)=stopped.
2. It has more than one syllable and the final one is stressed, e.g. admit=admitted.
In British English, travel and cancel are exceptions to this rule: travelled.
3. We do not double 'w', 'x' or 'y' at the end of words: play – played; snow - snowed.

Verbs ending in consonant+y, change the 'y' to an 'i' and add 'ed', e.g. carry=carried.
